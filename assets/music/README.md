# Recommended royalty-free music sources

For a sensory-friendly nursery rhyme YouTube video, use music that is soft, bright, and warm without being too intense. The following sources are commonly safe for YouTube use when you keep to their licensing terms:

- Pixabay Music: https://pixabay.com/music/
- Mixkit: https://mixkit.co/free-stock-music/
- Free Music Archive: https://freemusicarchive.org/

Recommended style:
- gentle piano
- acoustic lullaby
- subtle orchestral sparkle
- happy children’s music loop
- soft ukulele or marimba for playful scenes

Best practice:
- Keep the volume lower than narration by 4-8 dB.
- Use a 10-20 second intro loop.
- Use a gentle fade in/out at transitions.
- Avoid loud bass or sudden tempos.

Suggested download approach:
- Download the music to `assets/music/`
- Rename the files to something clean and consistent
- Add them to the final render timeline in the same order as the rhymes

Examples:
- `assets/music/soft_piano_intro.mp3`
- `assets/music/playful_marimba_loop.mp3`
- `assets/music/pastel_orchestra_loop.mp3`

Use only tracks that explicitly allow YouTube commercial use, or where you have a commercial license.
