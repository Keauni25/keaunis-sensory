# Storyboard and Timing

Total target length: ~5 minutes (~300 seconds). The following breakdown assigns approximate durations and key characters per scene. Animation pacing is baby-friendly: slow entrances, clear focal points, large simple motions, warm easing curves, and 3–4 second scene transitions where appropriate.

1) Opening title (7s)
   - Visuals: large friendly star character appears, soft zoom in, title "Keaunis Sensory"
   - Audio: gentle chime + intro music

2) Twinkle Twinkle Little Star (45s)
   - Visuals: star character (friendly face) drifts in; soft sparkles; small animal friends (baby bird, lamb) look up
   - Lyrics: full first verse and short reprise
   - Characters: Star, Baby Bird, Lamb

3) Old MacDonald Had A Farm (55s)
   - Visuals: farm background; cow, pig, chicken, duck hop in and sing short repeated lines
   - Lyrics: shortened to main chorus and one animal per line
   - Characters: Cow, Pig, Chicken, Duck

4) Baa Baa Black Sheep (40s)
   - Visuals: gentle pasture with three playful sheep; flocking animation and soft camera pan
   - Lyrics: full short verse repeated with call-and-response
   - Characters: Sheep (3)

5) Itsy Bitsy Spider (40s)
   - Visuals: close-up on spout; spider character climbs; rain animation washes down then sun returns
   - Lyrics: full single verse with animated web
   - Characters: Spider

6) Humpty Dumpty (40s)
   - Visuals: anthropomorphic Humpty (cute egg) sits on wall, playful wobble, exaggerated soft fall with smiley split and bounce animation—non-violent, cheerful slapstick
   - Lyrics: one verse with gentle recovery scene
   - Characters: Humpty (egg)

7) Hickory Dickory Dock (28s)
   - Visuals: big friendly clock tower, mouse runs up and down; clock hands animate to beat
   - Lyrics: chorus repeated twice
   - Characters: Mouse, Clock

8) Closing montage / outro (25s)
   - Visuals: all characters assemble in a big colorful group, wave, and star sparkles; end card with subscribe call-to-action and gentle fade out.
   - Audio: music swells then fades; friendly voiceover says "Thanks for watching!"

Notes on pacing:
- Each lyric line lines up with simple character motions. No fast cuts; transitions last 2–4 seconds so infants can track movement.
- Use bright primary colors with high contrast for key elements and softer pastels for backgrounds.

Assets in this repo:
- assets/svg/: friendly vector animal characters (lamb, cow, pig, spider, humpty egg, mouse, star)
- 2d/scene.js uses simple procedural animals but SVG assets can be swapped in for higher fidelity
- 3d/ contains Blender script for low-poly versions

If you want specific design tweaks (more pastel vs saturated, eye shapes, or different animals) tell me which ones to adjust and I’ll update the SVGs and scene placement to match.
